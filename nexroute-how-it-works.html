NexRoute how one delivery works

Fixed tape route only. No map. RFID names the node. The line moves the robot.

X → Y ONLY
Saved turns at J1: Left to X · Left to Y · Straight back to Base. A tag that is not the expected one is ignored. The robot waits for a person at X and at Y.

Track — 3-way junction

What happens, in order

  1. 1
    Robot waits on the wide black marker. State: STANDBY. It does not move.
  2. 2
    Phone opens 192.168.4.1 and calls X → Y. Any other pair is rejected.
  3. 3
    IR array follows the tape. At the key ring, J1 is recognized and the robot turns left toward X.
  4. 4
    Card 1 means X. Robot stops. Sender puts the item on the open tray and presses Item Loaded.
  5. 5
    Robot spins 180°, follows the tape back, reads J1 again, and turns left toward Y.
  6. 6
    Card 2 means Y. Robot stops. Receiver takes the item and presses Received.
  7. 7
    Robot spins 180°, goes straight through J1, sees all 8 sensors black, and returns to STANDBY.

If something is within 15 cm, motors stop and the buzzer sounds. When the path is clear, the same leg continues. Cancel on the page stops the job immediately.
